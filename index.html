FIT2179 Data Visualisation 2

Australia Runs on Coffee

Exploring how café access, coffee habits, spending, and brand presence shape Australia’s café culture.

12,727 café locations processed
13 linked visual idioms
5 data themes combined

1. Australia’s café landscape

Raw café counts mostly reveal population size. The first three views normalise, locate, and rank café access to show availability more fairly.

Key takeaway

ACT and Tasmania rise once café counts are compared with population. This changes the story from “where are the most cafés?” to “where is café access strongest?”

2. Who drinks coffee?

Coffee culture is behavioural as well as geographic. Age affects frequency, preferred drink type, and whether coffee is made at home or bought outside.

3. Spending and trends

Spending views show how cafés, takeaway formats, pods and home coffee sit within the wider coffee economy.

4. Relationships

The next charts show movement and relationships rather than isolated totals.

5. Final insight

The combined scorecard summarises the wider café culture pattern by blending density, spending, and brand presence.

Sources and acknowledgement

Datasets compiled from OpenStreetMap café locations, ABS National/State/Territory Population, ABS Monthly Household Spending Indicator, Roy Morgan public coffee consumption findings, Canstar Blue coffee shop ratings, and coffee brand/franchise store information. Some intermediate tables are cleaned and derived from these sources for visualisation. Generative AI was used to support layout, text editing, and Vega-Lite code drafting.
